**IBM x Kerala Government Hackathon 2026**

**Challenge 7: Backwater Cargo Exchange**

## Background

Kerala possesses one of India's largest inland waterway networks. However, most goods continue to move by road despite waterways often being cheaper and more environmentally friendly.

One major challenge is the lack of visibility between cargo owners and boat operators.

## Challenge

Build a marketplace-like platform that connects cargo owners with inland water transport operators.

## Target Users

- Cargo owners
- Boat operators
- Logistics companies
- Inland waterway service providers

## Suggested Features

- Cargo posting
- Boat availability listing
- Matching recommendations
- Schedule planning
- Cost comparison

## Example Scenario

A construction company needs to transport building materials from Kochi to Alappuzha and wants to compare road transport with waterway options.

## Impact

Promote sustainable logistics and increase utilization of Kerala's waterways.
