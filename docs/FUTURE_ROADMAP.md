# Future roadmap

1. **Validated Kerala pilot:** partner with operators, terminal managers and relevant authority; validate draft/clearance/depth and hazardous-cargo processes, tariff models and real capacity. Replace fixtures with provenance-backed snapshots and freshness checks.
2. **Production tenancy:** managed identity/SSO, user invitations, custom permission scopes, commercial confidentiality policies, audit retention, PostgreSQL/Alembic, backup/restore, real contention tests.
3. **Intake integrations:** authenticated Malayalam STT evaluation, multilingual document corpus, resource-limited OCR workers, XLSX sheets, approved WhatsApp Business channel and human-reviewed extraction. Measure extraction on real labeled examples; do not fabricate accuracy.
4. **Execution:** real vessel GPS, verified route notices, tide/lock/weather feeds, terminal truck appointments, multi-berth/equipment calendars, crew scheduling, verified photos/QR cargo labels and signed/OTP proof of delivery.
5. **Optimization:** fleet-wide OR-Tools routing, demand-confirmed recurring services, actual tariff benchmarks, cold-chain compatibility, broader return-corridor overlap and coordinated pool recovery. Train ETA/risk models only after sufficient real voyage evidence.
6. **Government decision support:** verified corridor demand, unmet-demand attribution, audited modal shift, surveyed infrastructure gaps and scenario sensitivity. Prototype opportunity scores remain advisory.
7. **Commercial integrations:** transaction fee, operator subscription, enterprise 3PL SaaS and scheduled-service tools; production payments/escrow, insurance and authorized regulatory-document integrations only with real partnerships.

Potential business models are hypotheses, not validated revenue. Real flood/emergency workflows require verified observations, authorization and operational protocols; the current simulation is only a demo scenario.
