# Nine-slide presentation outline

1. **Kerala freight problem:** fragmented demand/capacity and road dependence. Use source-grounded corridor context; avoid invented market-volume claims.
2. **Why manual visibility fails:** an available vessel can still fail draft, terminal handling, deadline or compliance. Show Deep Blue's deterministic failure.
3. **Jalayatra solution:** freight understanding → constraints → optimization → approval → execution → measurable impact in one platform.
4. **User ecosystem:** shippers, operators, captains, terminals, 3PL, safety/finance and government; fifteen workspaces on a shared freight twin.
5. **AI + optimization architecture:** reviewed multilingual/document intake and actual local OCR; deterministic physical truth; OR-Tools pooling/fleet assignment; Dijkstra routes; dated services/resource calendars; no safety override.
6. **Hero workflow:** 80 t cement + 58 t steel on a 150 t barge; review intake, three modes, rejection, 92% pooling, 62 t return opportunity, booking/tracking.
7. **Measured prototype results:** read generated `measured-results.json`; show calculated utilization and booking-derived costs/carbon. Separate suggestions, selected plans and completed delivery.
8. **Government and business impact:** transparent demand gaps, terminal/corridor intelligence, equivalent avoided road movements and possible transaction/subscription/3PL business models. All economics are prototype assumptions.
9. **Scale and closing:** validated operator/authority pilot, verified navigation data, real identity/integrations, PostgreSQL concurrency and multi-tour fleet routing. “Understand the freight. Prove the journey. Make every voyage count.”
