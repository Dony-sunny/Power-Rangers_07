# Presentation outline

1. Problem: cargo owners and inland boat operators lack visibility.
2. Users: three marketplace workspaces with structured operational teams.
3. Posting and availability: review complete cargo/load units and dated vessel availability.
4. Comparison: show equivalent delivered cost, constraints and schedule.
5. Commitments: exact owner quotes, operator acceptance and required resource responses.
6. Execution: one shared voyage, individual door ETAs, warehouse handover and receiver receipts.
7. Operational tools: import, evidence, crew, maintenance and finance.
8. Public context and government analysis: source-labeled reference/weather, then teammate's government work.
9. Results, boundaries and next pilot steps.

## Success metrics

Keep proposed, confirmed and completed tonnes separate. Distinguish long-haul truck equivalents from actual connecting truck trips, utilization from modeled contribution, estimated emissions from measured emissions, and app milestone timing from external measured service reliability. Never call a simulated provider response an external dispatch confirmation.

If acceptance/provider steps cannot be demonstrated, use a D1 comparison-only or D1+D2 scheduling/ownership fallback and state exactly which commitments remain unconfirmed. Test evidence and screenshots must come from the current implementation; the earlier [implementation review](IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md) is historical.
